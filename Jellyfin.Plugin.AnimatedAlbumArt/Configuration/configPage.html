Animated Album Art

Put a silent looping video named cover-motion.mp4 or cover-motion.mov in an album folder, next to its static cover. Changes are picked up immediately; no library scan is needed.

When off, every album reports no motion artwork.
Comma separated, most preferred first. Supported: .mp4, .m4v, .mov, .webm.
Loads a small script into the web client that plays motion artwork on album pages. Browsers must reload the page after changing this.
Creates silent H.264 copies in Jellyfin’s cache; originals stay in your library. Generation runs in the background. Use Scheduled Tasks → Prepare artwork cache to prepare albums ahead of time.
Oldest copies are removed when the limit is reached. Copies expire after 30 days; they can be generated again.
